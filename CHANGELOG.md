# Changelog

| Version | Date | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| **1.8.0** | 2026-09-30 | **Combine…** in the library's selection bar: select 2 to 4 routes and open *Combine* with them (the button says why when fewer or more are selected). The About page describes the new combine steps. The screenshot script (`docs/screenshots/take_screenshots.py`) drives the new combine screen (two parts, one connection, accepted) |
| **1.7.0** | 2026-09-30 | **Combine: check each connection.** In the *Ride* step every connection between two parts is routed on its own and listed: *Accept* it, or open *Other options…* for BRouter's other routes (up to four, the same route twice shown once; also drawn dotted on the map, click one to pick it), a straight line, or one of your places to ride through. When BRouter can't route a connection, its options open with the straight line to fall back on. *Save* and *Download GPX* wait until every connection is accepted (*Accept all*), and use exactly the accepted lines: nothing is routed again, also not when you ride the result the other way. A connection keeps its choice while the part ends it joins and the routing options stay the same |
| **1.6.0** | 2026-09-30 | **Combine, redone as four steps**, for 2 to 4 routes: **Routes** (tick them in a list you narrow down with the Browse panel and a name search, without touching the library's filters, or click them on the map), **Parts** (on each route, click where a part starts and ends and drag its ends; ⇄ turns it round, *long way* goes round a loop through its start; several parts per route, at most 6), **Connect** (click the end ■ of a part, then the start ▶ of the next; *Auto: loop* / *Auto: point to point* pick the order with the shortest gaps; the screen says what is still missing and warns when connections cross) and **Ride** (connectors routed by BRouter, through a place if you like, *Your ride* step by step, save or download). The link keeps the routes, parts, connections and step. The three patterns (*Out on A, back on B*, *Two crossings*, *A, then B*) are gone; old links with two routes still open them. Under the hood: BRouter's alternative routes (0–3), connections routed one at a time and approved connectors kept as they are (for the next release) |
| **1.5.0** | 2026-09-29 | **Import accepts rerouter zips:** a set made with *Export set…* (or a backup), from this or another rerouter, dropped on the Import screen is added as a set with its details (activity, tags, notes, ratings, places, collections, areas) instead of being unpacked into bare GPX files; nothing in the library is replaced, routes it already has are skipped, and a link shows the new routes on the map |
